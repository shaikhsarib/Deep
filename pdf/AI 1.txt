## 4. Super-agent architecture
Evaluate the article's mechanisms/trade-offs:
- **Router/Workers:** persistent Router handles interaction, global context and dispatch; isolated Workers execute concurrently/report back; Workers may spawn subagents/teams.
- **Triggers:** chat/email/calendar/GitHub/CI; deterministic Fast Path filters noise, semantic LLM Slow Path judges candidates; Thread subscriptions, pass-through and batching.
- **Context:** Inline relevant content, Spill excess behind references, Chain of Recall across task/thread/session links.
- **Full-duplex:** async start/stop long tools without blocking new input; completion as events; adapt output for chat/email/GitHub/work UIs.
- **Persistence:** separate compute, durable state and execution environments; cloud runtime, DB state, local/cloud sandboxes or endpoint daemons, BYOM and reconnect recovery.
- **Watchdog:** independent monitoring of infrastructure and Worker progress/goal drift; recover, retry, stop or escalate.
- **Speculative execution:** background action from signals; assess waste, cancellation, risk and permissions.

Assess scale, concurrency, memory, A2A, endpoint trust and security; separate MVP from later stages.
